## Caveats

- A match is a name match, not proof. Exact normalized name plus a trade-compatible Overture category inside Chicago is `high`; same name outside the city, a missing category, or a close-but-not-exact name is `medium`; names shared by several different businesses are dropped as ambiguous.
- Verify every contact before outreach (open the website, check the name and trade). Owner names from the business-owner records are public filings and are a name suggestion only.
- Overture phone numbers can be a company's main line or a mobile number; call business lines manually only and never autodial or text (docs/14, section 8).
- Chicago business licenses cover only businesses that need a city license, so many contractors and most single-property LLCs have no record there.
