# Deliverable 1 — Product Vision

*v1, 2026-11-14. Labels: [V] verified, [E] estimate, [H] hypothesis, [S] speculative — see `00-strategic-assessment.md`.*

## Problem

Local service businesses and trade contractors mostly acquire customers reactively: referrals, inbound calls, ads, bid invitations. The information that predicts demand — permits, violations, licenses, property sales, zoning filings, public bids — is public but scattered across agencies, formats and portals, is messy (truncated names, self-reported costs), and arrives at different points in a project's life. Small businesses don't have the time or skills to turn it into a sales pipeline; large ones pay Dodge/ConstructConnect-class tools that are built for estimators and bid rooms, not for owner-operators.

## Vision

**An intelligence engine that tells a local business who is about to need what it sells, why, who decides, and what to say — and then measures whether it worked.** One shared engine, with industry configuration determining which signals, scores, terminology and recommendations each customer sees.

Long-term surfaces (in order of when they should exist): weekly report → CRM sync → daily briefing email → internal AI assistant → customer-facing AI advisor → industry dashboards → agency unified reporting.

## Target customers (first two years)

| Segment | What they buy | Why they'd pay | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial subcontractors (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, concrete/masonry, fire protection) | GC-relationship and market intelligence; pre-permit project signals | Work is won through GC relationships; the data shows who to target | [H] |
| Construction suppliers, distributors, equipment rental | Contractor-level buying signals ("this contractor just pulled a $1.2M permit 3 miles from your yard") | Have sales reps and sales budgets; buyer named on record | [H] |
| Post-construction and new-business services (commercial cleaning, low-voltage/security/AV, signage, pest, waste, telecom, insurance) | New-building and new-business opportunity feed with contacts | Not named on permits → the lead is genuinely early | [H] |
| Residential-facing trades with compelled demand (porch, masonry, windows, roofing) | Violation + permit-history signals, direct-mail ready | Clear trigger; but individual owners → mail only | [H] |
| Agency clients (any local business) | Ads/social/GBP + the intelligence report as the differentiator | Wants one partner for customer acquisition | [H] |

## Value proposition

- **For a commercial sub:** "Know which GCs to court, which ones are rotating subs, and which projects are coming before bid lists close — with the contact and a drafted intro."
- **For a supplier/rental company:** "Every week: the contractors near you who just started projects in your category, with the project, value, location and a reason to call."
- **For a service provider:** "New buildings and new businesses in your area that will need your service in the next 30–90 days."
- **For every customer:** every item is sourced, timestamped, explains why it was recommended, and separates **confirmed facts** from **inferences**.

## Principles

1. **Evidence over volume.** 25 verified, relevant items beat 500 unfiltered permits.
2. **Every claim is sourced.** Link to the original record; show freshness; never fabricate contacts.
3. **Confirmed vs inferred is a first-class field**, not a footnote.
4. **Measure outcomes.** A feature exists only if it moves relevance, meetings, wins or cost.
5. **Humans approve outbound.** The system prepares; people send.
6. **Shared engine, configurable verticals.** Industry modules are data (taxonomies, rules, weights, templates), not separate apps.

## What success looks like

- 12 months [H]: 20–40 paying customers in Chicago across 1–2 buyer types, >70% 3-month retention, attributable meetings and wins for most customers, cost per accepted lead measured and falling.
- 36 months [S]: multiple metros, self-serve tier, outcome-trained scoring, agency and software sharing one CRM and one attribution view.

## Non-goals (for now)

- Nationwide coverage. Multiple metros. Native mobile apps.
- A full CRM. Automated sending, texting or AI calling.
- A polished dashboard before the report is retained by paying customers.
