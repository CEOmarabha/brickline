# Deliverable 6 — Business Model (incl. Agency Service Catalog)

*v1, 2026-11-14. All margins and hours are [E] estimates with stated assumptions; market prices are [V]/[V3] with sources. Replace estimates with time-tracking data from the first 3 clients.*

## 1. Summary

- **Lead with managed sales intelligence** ($750–$1,500/mo after a discounted pilot). It's the product being validated, needs little software, and its cost is mostly review time that becomes shared as customers in one cluster grow.
- **Agency packages are viable at the proposed prices only with templated, AI-assisted production** and clear scope limits. Do not bundle the full weekly intelligence report into Business Growth at $1,499 — it erases the margin.
- **Ad management:** keep $350–$400 entry fees but cap the included spend and switch to a percentage above the cap; charge setup and creative separately.
- **Self-serve SaaS** at $149–$499 comes later, once the managed product proves value and the work is mostly automated. It will be compared with $27–$129 permit feeds, so it must sell relationship intelligence and outcomes, not alerts.

## 3. Unit economics by offer

Assumptions [E]: blended delivery labor $30/hour (mix of offshore/contract specialists and US account management); HighLevel Unlimited amortized across 10 clients (~$30/client); design/scheduling/reporting tools ~$40/client; sales commission 10% of first-year revenue, shown separately.

### 3.1 Package A — Business Essentials, proposed $799/mo

| Work | Hours/mo |
| --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile (weekly posts, photos, Q&A, review responses) | 3 |
| Basic social, 2 platforms, ~12 posts | 4–6 |
| Reputation monitoring and review requests | 1 |
| Monthly report | 1 |
| Account management | 1–2 |
| Monthly market brief from the engine (shared across clients in the same trade) | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **10.5–13.5** |

Cost ≈ $315–$405 labor + ~$70 tools → **gross margin ~40–52%** before commission. With AI-assisted drafting and templates cutting content time by a third, ~50–58%. **Verdict:** viable; keep scope tight (posts count, platforms, revisions).

### 3.2 Package B — Business Growth, proposed $1,499/mo

| Work | Hours/mo |
| --- | --- |
| Everything in Essentials (expanded social, 3 platforms) | 13–17 |
| CRM setup amortized + pipeline/automation upkeep | 3–4 |
| Appointment booking workflow, follow-up sequences | 2 |
| Advanced reporting | 1–2 |
| **Subtotal** | **19–25** |
| *If full weekly intelligence report included (50 items/week, unshared)* | *+7–14* |

Without the weekly report: $570–$750 labor + ~$100 tools → **margin ~43–55%**. With it: **~15–40%** → not sustainable. **Verdict:** include the monthly brief + a capped "top 10 opportunities per week"; sell full managed intelligence separately.

### 3.3 Ad management add-ons (proposed $350–$400/platform)

Ongoing effort ~3–6 hours/platform/month at small spend → $90–$180 labor. Margins are fine at small budgets but effort grows with spend and with creative needs (Meta/TikTok/YouTube need frequent new creative; video is the expensive part).

Recommended structure:

| Platform | Monthly fee | Included spend | Above the cap | One-time setup | Creative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search | $350 | up to $1,500 | 15% of spend | $400–$600 | Ad copy included |
| Meta | $400 | up to $1,500 | 15% of spend | $400–$600 | 4 static/carousel per month included; video extra |
| TikTok | $350 | up to $1,500 | 15% of spend | $400–$600 | Video production quoted separately |
| YouTube | $350 | up to $2,000 | 12% of spend | $400–$600 | Video production quoted separately |

Rationale: a flat fee with no cap loses money as clients scale; pure percentage looks bad at small budgets; hybrid matches effort and stays competitive with the market median.

### 3.4 Managed sales intelligence (recommended first product)

| | Pilot (months 1–3) | Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $250–$500/mo, or free for the first month in exchange for weekly feedback and outcome data | $750–$1,500/mo depending on items/week and markets |
| Deliverable | Weekly report: 20–50 reviewed items with evidence, reason, contact, drafted outreach; monthly market brief; CRM entry | Same + daily alerts for "call today" items + quarterly review |
| Cost to deliver [E] | 7–14 hours/month unshared ($210–$420) + contacts $20–$50 + infra ~$10 | Falls toward 3–6 hours as research is shared across customers in the cluster |
| Gross margin [E] | Negative to ~30% (investment) | ~55–75% at $1,000 with shared research |

Value check [H]: a commercial sub that wins one extra $100k job a year at 10% net margin makes $10k; a supplier that wins one contractor account worth $50k/year at 20% gross margin makes $10k. A $1,000/month service needs roughly one such win per year to pay back — plausible, which is exactly what the pilots must prove.
